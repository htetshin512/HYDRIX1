ABOUT THESE POLICIES

Understanding our website policies

HYDRIX is an educational website created as part of a Year 9 ICT project. It provides information about drought, water shortages, water conservation and preparedness.

These policies explain how the website is intended to operate, how external resources are used, and the responsibilities of visitors accessing its content.

These statements should be reviewed whenever HYDRIX introduces new services, forms, analytics or data-handling features.

DOCUMENT 01

Privacy Policy

This section explains what information HYDRIX may handle when someone visits or interacts with the website.

01

Information we collect

HYDRIX is designed primarily as an informational website. Visitors can access its educational pages without creating an account or providing identifying information.

The current educational features are intended to work directly in the visitor's browser. If visitors choose to contact the project team, any information they voluntarily provide should be handled only for that communication.

02

Browser storage and preferences

Some website features may use browser storage to remember local preferences. For example, this page uses localStorage to remember whether a visitor has accepted the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

This agreement record stays in the visitor's browser. It is not, by itself, transmitted to a HYDRIX server. Visitors can withdraw their agreement using the control below or clear their browser's website storage.

03

External websites and services

HYDRIX contains links to research publications, educational organisations, image resources and other websites. When visitors open external links, the destination website's own privacy practices may apply.

The site also uses external resources such as Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons and Google Fonts. Loading resources from external providers can involve requests from the visitor's browser to those providers.

04

How information is used

HYDRIX's browser-based preferences are intended to support features such as remembering an accepted agreement. Any personal information voluntarily submitted through a future contact or communication service should be used only for its stated purpose.

This educational page does not implement an advertising, analytics or personal profiling system. Actual data practices may also depend on the hosting provider and any services connected to the site.

05

Data security and limitations

We aim to keep HYDRIX simple and avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. However, no website or internet connection can guarantee complete security.

Visitors should avoid submitting passwords, financial details or other sensitive information through educational contact channels.

06

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when HYDRIX introduces new features, changes how it handles information, or revises its website services.

Important changes should be explained on this page, along with an updated revision date. Visitors are encouraged to review the current version.

DOCUMENT 02

Terms of Service

These terms describe the intended use of HYDRIX and the responsibilities of visitors using its information and features.

01

Purpose of HYDRIX

HYDRIX is a student-developed educational resource focusing on drought awareness, water conservation and preparedness before water shortages.

Its purpose is to share accessible information and encourage responsible learning about environmental challenges.

02

Use of educational content

Visitors may explore HYDRIX for personal learning, school research and non-commercial educational purposes, subject to the rights of the original content owners.

Information should be interpreted carefully and checked against relevant primary sources when used in reports or important decisions.

03

Accuracy of information

We aim to present useful and accurate information based on publicly available educational and research resources. However, content may become outdated, incomplete or contain errors.

HYDRIX should not be treated as an official drought-warning service or a substitute for announcements from relevant authorities and experts.

04

Interactive educational features

HYDRIX may include interactive maps, charts, research guides and preparedness activities.

These features are created for learning and demonstration. Unless explicitly identified otherwise, they should not be understood as providing official assessments, personalised safety advice or real-time emergency warnings.

05

Copyright and third-party resources

HYDRIX may refer to research, photographs, software libraries and other materials owned by third parties. Their original copyright and licensing conditions continue to apply.

Website visitors should credit original sources and obtain permission where required before reproducing material. See our Citations & Credits page for research and resource links.

06

Responsible website use

Visitors should use HYDRIX respectfully and avoid activities intended to disrupt the website, interfere with its features or misuse any communication channels.

Visitors should also respect the rights and privacy of other people when sharing educational information.

07

Updates and availability

HYDRIX may revise its educational content, improve website features, change its policies or temporarily experience interruptions.

The project team does not guarantee that all pages, external links or interactive features will remain available at all times.
