01 / THE REGIONAL CONTEXT

Understanding Myanmar's Central Dry Zone

Myanmar's Central Dry Zone lies in the interior of the country and includes parts of Mandalay, Magway and Sagaing Regions. It generally receives less rainfall than many other areas of Myanmar.

The Dry Zone is not a desert. Rainfall arrives mainly during the monsoon, while the remaining months are considerably drier. The amount and timing of rainfall vary between locations and years.

Water availability depends on more than rainfall. Wells, reservoirs, rivers, irrigation systems and storage facilities all influence how communities access water.

02 / WATER AVAILABILITY

Why Water Shortages Happen

Different communities experience different water challenges. These three factors help explain why planning and water management matter.

01 / CLIMATE

Uneven Rainfall

Most rainfall occurs during the monsoon months. Delayed rain, long dry spells or unusually weak rainfall can reduce soil moisture and affect agriculture.

02 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Water Supply & Storage

Communities depend on wells, ponds, rivers and reservoirs. Poor maintenance, inadequate storage and limited distribution systems can worsen shortages.

03 / DEMAND

Community Water Needs

Homes, schools, farms and businesses use water for different purposes. Shortages can become more serious when demand rises or available supplies are not managed carefully.

04 / REGIONAL EXPLORER

Exploring Towns in Myanmar's Central Dry Zone

Although these regions share relatively dry climatic conditions, their water resources, agricultural activities and community needs are not exactly the same. Explore nine selected towns to understand their geography, water requirements, possible challenges and practical preparedness measures.

01
NORTHERN & CENTRAL MYANMAR

Sagaing Region

Monywa · Shwebo · Salingyi

03 TOWNS

Monywa is situated beside the Chindwin River in Sagaing Region. The river is an important geographical feature and a potential water source for surrounding communities. However, being near a river does not always guarantee reliable access to clean drinking water. Households, farms and businesses all need water throughout the year.

Water Needs Drinking, cooking, sanitation, farming and irrigation.
Potential Challenges Irregular rainfall, limited treated water and poorly maintained distribution systems.
Preparedness Inspect water facilities, protect stored drinking water, repair leaks and improve irrigation efficiency.

Shwebo is an important agricultural town in Sagaing Region. Farming depends on seasonal rainfall and suitable irrigation supplies. When rainfall arrives later than expected, farmers may need to adjust their planting activities and water management methods.

Water Needs Crop cultivation, irrigation, livestock care and households.
Potential Challenges Delayed monsoon rainfall, soil moisture loss and limited water storage.
Preparedness Maintain irrigation channels, monitor rainfall and use suitable water-saving methods.

Salingyi is located in lower Sagaing Region. Rural households and agricultural communities may depend on wells, ponds, stored rainwater and other local sources. Their reliability can change with seasonal rainfall, infrastructure conditions and water demand.

Water Needs Household drinking water, hygiene, cooking and agriculture.
Potential Challenges Limited storage, unreliable sources and possible water contamination.
Preparedness Inspect wells, protect water sources and use clean covered storage containers.
02
CENTRAL DRY ZONE

Mandalay Region

Myingyan · Nyaung-U · Kyaukpadaung

03 TOWNS

Myingyan is located in Mandalay Region and is associated with Myanmar's Central Dry Zone. Seasonal rainfall plays an important role in farming. Wells, reservoirs and irrigation facilities can provide additional water where available. Water management matters during long dry periods.

Water Needs Rain-fed farming, irrigated agriculture, livestock and households.
Potential Challenges Irregular rainfall, reduced soil moisture and inefficient irrigation.
Preparedness Maintain storage systems, conserve soil moisture and improve irrigation practices.

Nyaung-U is near the historic Bagan area in Mandalay Region. Water is needed by households, farms, accommodation facilities, businesses and public services. Reliable distribution and storage systems are important for managing demand during the drier months.

Water Needs Residents, farms, schools, businesses and visitor facilities.
Potential Challenges Seasonal demand, leaking pipes and insufficient water storage.
Preparedness Repair leaks, maintain storage facilities and encourage responsible water use.

Kyaukpadaung is situated near Mount Popa in Mandalay Region. Its surroundings include agricultural communities that depend on seasonal rainfall and available local water sources. Geography can influence water availability, even within the same township.

Water Needs Agriculture, livestock, households and community facilities.
Potential Challenges Unpredictable rainfall, declining soil moisture and limited storage.
Preparedness Protect ponds and reservoirs, inspect wells and improve soil moisture conservation.
03
CENTRAL & WESTERN MYANMAR

Magway Region

Pakokku · Chauk · Magway

03 TOWNS

Pakokku is located in northern Magway Region near the Ayeyarwady River. Although the river is an important water resource, nearby communities still need suitable systems to obtain, treat and distribute safe water. Dependable storage helps manage seasonal pressure.

Water Needs Households, crop production, sanitation and businesses.
Potential Challenges Unequal water access, damaged infrastructure and water quality concerns.
Preparedness Protect drinking-water sources, maintain tanks and identify alternative supplies.

Chauk is situated in Magway Region and experiences seasonal differences between wetter monsoon months and relatively dry periods. Homes, workplaces and agriculture need water throughout the year. Preparing before shortages can protect essential activities.

Water Needs Drinking, cooking, sanitation, workplaces and agriculture.
Potential Challenges Long dry spells, inefficient water use and limited storage.
Preparedness Inspect wells and tanks, repair leaks and prepare essential household water plans.

Magway is an important urban centre near the Ayeyarwady River. Homes, schools, healthcare facilities, businesses and agricultural communities all require dependable water. Reliable infrastructure is important because supply interruptions may affect public services and daily life.

Water Needs Homes, schools, hospitals, businesses and agriculture.
Potential Challenges High demand, ageing distribution systems and supply interruptions.
Preparedness Maintain distribution systems, repair leaks and prepare water arrangements for essential facilities.
UNDERSTANDING REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

Why Do Water Challenges Differ Between Towns?

Although these nine towns are in the broader Central Dry Zone, their water availability is influenced by different geographical and human factors. Towns near major rivers may have access to significant water resources, while others depend more heavily on wells, reservoirs, ponds and seasonal rainfall.

Farming communities may need additional irrigation during dry conditions. Urban areas require dependable water supplies for households, schools, businesses and healthcare facilities. Understanding these differences helps communities plan ahead.

06 / COMMUNITY RESILIENCE

Water Shortage Challenges and Preparedness

Water shortages can affect farming, household activities, health, education and local businesses. Communities with limited storage or unreliable sources may face greater difficulties during prolonged dry periods.

Preparing early through better water management, safe storage, regular maintenance and community awareness can help protect essential daily activities.

Reliable Water Access

Identify available sources such as wells, ponds, reservoirs and public systems. Inspect them regularly to detect damage and declining supplies.

Safe Drinking Water

Protect drinking water from contamination, use clean covered containers and follow reliable guidance on water treatment.

Agricultural Planning

Consider seasonal rainfall, crop requirements and irrigation supplies. Suitable methods can reduce unnecessary water loss.

Schools and Community Facilities

Schools, clinics and other facilities need reliable water for drinking, hygiene and sanitation. Storage and alternative supply plans are important.

Water Conservation and Maintenance

Repair leaking taps and pipes, inspect tanks and encourage efficient water use to reduce waste.

Community Awareness

Schools and community groups can share advice about water-saving habits, safe storage, local conditions and early preparedness.
